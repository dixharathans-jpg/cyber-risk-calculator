5. Methodology

  • Likelihood and impact are rated on the NIST scale: 1 Very Low, 2 Low, 3 Moderate, 4 High, 5 Very High.
  • Impact is the highest of the Confidentiality, Integrity and Availability ratings.
  • The risk level is looked up in NIST SP 800-30 Table I-2 (likelihood and impact combined).
  • Residual likelihood = ceil(likelihood x (1 - control effectiveness)), minimum 1.
  • Yearly loss: SLE = asset value x exposure factor, ALE = SLE x ARO, ALE after controls = ALE x (1 - control effectiveness).
  • All ratings and values are estimates for a fictional case study.
